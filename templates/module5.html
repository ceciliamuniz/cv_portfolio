Module 5 Part 2: Markerless Tracking Analysis

10 Scenarios for Tracking a Human Hand (Markerless OpenCV)
Comprehensive analysis of OpenCV's markerless tracking performance under various challenging conditions.

# Scenario Accuracy Challenge Observations
1 Rapid Hand Waving / Quick Gesture
Tracking hand while quickly waving side-to-side, causing motion blur 		65% High The tracker struggles with high velocity. The target pixel neighborhood shifts too far for the local search window, leading to consistent lag and eventual loss when motion blur occurs.
2 Hand Closing into a Fist
Tracking open palm then slowly curling into a fist 		78% Medium Lower accuracy due to drastic shape changes, violating the tracker's rigidity assumption. The tracker follows the open palm initially, but the bounding box doesn't adapt, including background pixels.
3 Occlusion by Another Object
Hand reaching for phone, partially hidden behind object 		85% Medium The tracker performs well initially but relies on prediction during occlusion. The bounding box starts to drift if the hand stops moving while hidden.
4 Lighting Change (Light to Shadow)
Moving hand from bright area into deep shadow zone 		55% High Rapid change in intensity gradients invalidates the initial correlation model. The texture features used for tracking change instantly, causing model failure.
5 Hand Against Textureless White Wall
Main scenario - tracking hand across flat white background 		90% Low High accuracy achieved. The hand provides excellent contrast against the white background, making edge detection much easier for the tracker.
6 Scale Change (Z-axis)
Pushing hand quickly away from camera (scale down) 		70% Medium Reduced accuracy since most basic markerless trackers are not highly scale-invariant and must constantly resize the tracking window, causing jitter and drift.
7 Tracking in Clutter
Hand hovering over busy background with complex features 		80% Medium The tracker risks feature ambiguity. Similar edge patterns and colors between hand/fingers and background can cause the bounding box to incorrectly lock onto background features.
8 Hand Rotating
Tracking back of hand, then rotating to show side view 		60% High Change in viewpoint drastically alters appearance and shape. Features visible at the back are completely hidden at side view, causing model failure.
9 Low Resolution / Feature Degradation
Tracking hand using very low resolution input 		75% Medium High speed due to low data processing, but lack of detailed texture makes the model weak. Tracker relies only on large boundary gradients and confuses hand with large nearby objects.
10 Initialization During Object Motion
Starting tracker while hand is already in motion (waving) 		45% High Very low accuracy. The initial tracking model is built from a blurred/distorted image, leading to poor model quality. Tracker immediately drifts and often fails within first frames.
